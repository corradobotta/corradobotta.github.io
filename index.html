I am a researcher working at the intersection of entrepreneurial finance, venture capital, and applied econometrics. My work uses quantitative and machine-learning methods to study how capital is allocated to private firms, how private-market investors are compensated for risk, and how policy shapes financial markets. I hold a PhD in Analytics for Economics and Management from the Università degli Studi di Brescia.
