My work focuses on venture capital and private equity. Using quantitative and machine-learning methods, I study how capital is allocated to private firms, how private-market investors are compensated for risk, and how policy shapes financial markets. I hold a PhD in Analytics for Economics and Management from the Università degli Studi di Brescia.
