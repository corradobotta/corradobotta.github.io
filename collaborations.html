Collaborations

I welcome enquiries in the areas below. For any of them, please write to corrado.botta@unibocconi.it.

Talks

I am available for seminars and conference talks on topics close to my research and writing: financial risk, applied econometrics and private markets.

Consulting and analysis

I am available for selected quantitative projects, such as the design and validation of risk models, the econometric analysis of financial data, and valuation.

Partnerships

My articles explain methods of econometrics and quantitative finance from first principles, with the code to reproduce every result. I am open to partnerships with companies whose data or software are relevant to this work.

Research

I welcome research collaborations in entrepreneurial finance, venture capital and applied econometrics. My papers and working papers are listed on the Research page.
